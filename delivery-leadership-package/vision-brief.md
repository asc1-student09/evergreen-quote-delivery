# Evergreen Quote Vision Brief

## Who is the customer?

The primary customer is a first-time insurance shopper, often a new renter or homeowner, looking for a quick estimate without creating an account or providing personal information.

## What pain does Evergreen Quote remove?

Many insurance websites require lengthy forms before displaying pricing information. Evergreen Quote removes this friction by providing an estimated monthly premium in under a minute.

## What does success look like at the end of the week?

- Responsive application on desktop and mobile
- Auto, Home, and Renters coverage options
- Working premium calculation
- Evergreen branding applied
- Merged to main through a reviewed pull request
- Green CI run

## What are we not doing this week?

- Real insurance pricing engine
- Customer accounts
- Saved quotes
- Email capture
- Payment processing
- Backend services or databases
