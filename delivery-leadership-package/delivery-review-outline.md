# Slide 3 — Two Key Decisions

## Decision #1
### Prioritize Core Functionality Over Additional Content

**Decision**
Focus on quote request functionality and responsive design instead of additional marketing content and design enhancements.

**Why**
Customers must be able to request a quote and use the application successfully on any device. Additional content can be added later.

---

## Decision #2
### No-Go Recommendation During Incident Investigation

**Decision**
Pause additional merges when a customer-facing pricing defect and CI failure were introduced in the incident scenario.

**Why**
Protecting customers and understanding risk was more important than maintaining delivery speed.

# Slide 5 — What I'd Do Differently Next Round

## Lessons Learned

### 1. Build leadership artifacts earlier

Completing the risk register, status updates, and decision documentation earlier would reduce end-of-week pressure.

### 2. Review risks more frequently

A formal risk review at the end of each day would help surface issues sooner.

### 3. Validate CI earlier

Enabling CI earlier in the delivery cycle would provide faster feedback and reduce integration risk.

### 4. Use AI intentionally

LibertyGPT accelerated content creation, but human review remained necessary to ensure authenticity and accuracy.

## Final Reflection

This capstone reinforced that delivery leadership is not about writing code. It is about prioritization, communication, risk management, stakeholder alignment, and making informed decisions when new information emerges.
